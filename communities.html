Picayune

Largest city in Pearl River County

  • Picayune had 11,885 residents in the 2020 Census, making it the largest city in the county.
  • It sits about 45 miles from New Orleans, Gulfport-Biloxi and Hattiesburg, so some residents commute for work.
  • Most of the city is in the Picayune School District, with a small part in the Pearl River County School District.

If you are buying or selling in Picayune

  • Check whether a property is inside the city limits. It can affect utilities, services and taxes.
  • Ask about the school district for the exact address, since a small part of the city is served by a different district.

Sources: U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts · Wikipedia: Picayune. Checked October 2026.

Poplarville

County seat and home of the Holliday office

  • Poplarville was incorporated in 1886 and became the county seat in 1890. The current courthouse was built in 1918.
  • Its population was 2,833 in the 2020 Census.
  • Pearl River Community College, founded in 1909, is here. The town is also known for the annual Blueberry Jubilee.

If you are buying or selling in Poplarville

  • Much of the area around town is rural, so ask about acreage, access and well or septic systems.
  • Holliday Real Estate has its office at 1729 S Main St. Stop by or call.

Sources: Mississippi Gulf Coast Heritage · Wikipedia: Poplarville. Checked October 2026.

Carriere

Mostly rural, along Highway 11

  • Carriere is an unincorporated community, so there is no city limit. Its ZIP code is 39426.
  • Pearl River Central High School (grades 9-12) is on Highway 11 in Carriere, part of the Pearl River County School District.
  • The 39426 ZIP code area had about 17,500 residents in the 2020 Census. That figure covers a wide rural area, not a town boundary.

If you are buying or selling in Carriere

  • Many properties here sit on larger lots. Ask about septic, wells, road access and a current survey.
  • Confirm the school district for the specific address before you decide.

Sources: Wikipedia: Carriere · Pearl River Central High School · ZIP-Codes.com. Checked October 2026.

Nicholson

South of Picayune, near the Louisiana line

  • Nicholson is an unincorporated community with ZIP code 39463. Its post office has operated since 1884.
  • The area was part of Hancock County until 1908, when it became part of Pearl River County.

If you are buying or selling in Nicholson

  • Parts of the county near the Pearl River can be flood-prone. Check any property on the FEMA flood map and ask about flood insurance before making an offer.
  • Ask about road access, septic and drainage on larger lots.

Sources: Wikipedia: Nicholson · MS Railroads: Nicholson. Checked October 2026.

Ask Elijah about Nicholson

McNeill

Small community on Highway 11

  • McNeill is an unincorporated community with ZIP code 39457. Its post office has served the area since 1891.
  • Pearl River Central schools are nearby in Carriere, along Highway 11.
  • In February 2024 the Mississippi Economic Development Council announced an award for the McNeill Community Park revitalization.

If you are buying or selling in McNeill

  • This is a quiet, spread-out area. Land and acreage questions come up often, so ask about boundaries, utilities and access.
  • Ask about the school district for the exact address.

Sources: MS Railroads: McNeill · Poplarville Democrat · Wikipedia: McNeill. Checked October 2026.

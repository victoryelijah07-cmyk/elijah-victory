I'm Elijah Victory.

I'm a Realtor® with Holliday Real Estate, licensed in Mississippi, and I work with buyers and sellers across Pearl River County.

Before real estate, I worked in construction and property maintenance, and I started and ran my own waste removal business. I studied business and marketing management. That mix taught me how properties hold up, how to run something on my own, and how to get back to people quickly.

My slogan is Choose Victory. To me it means doing the work, being straight with people, and helping you make a decision you feel good about.

I was born and raised in the Picayune and Slidell area, so this is home. I know the roads, the neighborhoods and the people.
